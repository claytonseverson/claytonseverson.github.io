Atlas

Atlas is a private tool used only by its owner, Clayton Severson, to keep a personal archive of his own Gmail and Google Drive on storage he controls.

Privacy policy

Atlas is not offered to the public. Its only user is its owner.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail (to read and organize messages) and the owner's Google Drive (read-only).
  • How data is used: solely to copy the owner's own messages and files to the owner's private storage and to search them for the owner.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone else, and none is used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Atlas's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
